The city never sleeps, and neither does the work of keeping it in order. Every morning a secretary opens the mail, sorts the reports and answers the calls before the first meeting begins. A good assistant is quick with the keyboard and quicker with the mind. When a message arrives that must be sent at once, there is no time to look for the right keys. The fingers must already know the way. Practice makes the hands steady, and steady hands finish the page before the clock runs out. So keep your eyes on the line, keep your rhythm even, and do not stop to correct what cannot be undone. Move forward one letter at a time, and the gap will close.
